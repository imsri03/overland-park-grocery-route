FreshRoute Overland Park • 3-Sided Ecosystem

One unified platform connecting Customers, Merchants, and Delivery Agents

🛒

1. Customer Shop (Ordering)

Customers pick an Indian grocery store nearby (Royal Spices, Reliance Mart, KC Indian Mart, Taj Groceries, TT Asian Grocery), browse live inventory & prices, and check out into one of two daily delivery batches — same-day 4–9 PM or next-morning.

🏬

2. Merchant Studio (Fulfillment & Catalog)

Merchants login to their store depot, review live incoming orders ("who ordered what"), check items off an itemized packing checklist, verify thermal bags for perishables, and request driver pickup. Merchants also edit product prices & inventory in real time.

🚚

3. Driver Maps (Navigation & Optimization)

Delivery agents pick up packed orders from Overland Park merchants, solve optimal multi-stop routes using 2-opt TSP solving, follow turn-by-turn navigation, and complete doorstep deliveries with live GPS simulation.
